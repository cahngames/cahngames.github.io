How do I play?

Your moth circles the lamp on two orbits. Tap anywhere to hop between them. Collect sparks, avoid thorns, and get close to thorns without touching them for bonus points.

How do lives work?

You have 5 lives and each run uses one. A life comes back every 20 minutes, even while the game is closed. You can also refill with coins or watch an optional ad for one extra life.

I bought coins but didn't get them.

Close and reopen Mothlight with an internet connection; unfinished purchases are delivered automatically. If they still don't arrive, email us with your order number from Google Play or the App Store.

How do I restore the starter pack on a new device?

Open Settings in the game (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases. On Android this also happens automatically.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it, so keep the app installed when you change settings.

How do I turn off personalised ads or analytics?

Open Settings in the game and switch Personalised ads & analytics off. Read more in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game to remove local data. To delete your leaderboard score and analytics data, email us and we'll handle it.
